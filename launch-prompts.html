Launch Prompts

30-day sequence

Create a 30-day FedPromptly launch sequence. For each day provide a small public action, one piece of content, a target audience, a factual proof point or learning goal, and a CTA. Use participation and transparency rather than fake scarcity. Leave dates as placeholders when no launch date is supplied.

60-second trailer

Write a 60-second FedPromptly trailer in four beats: the internet gave people the ability to create; building can still feel complicated; FedPromptly is a place for builders; Learn. Create. Deploy. Share. Include voiceover, screen text, scene direction, sound, and exact timing. Do not claim scale or success that is not verified.

Founder note

Write a founder note that explains what changed from the first idea to the current ecosystem. Include one belief, one thing that broke, one thing being tested, and one invitation to help. Avoid resume language and grand predictions.
