Frequently Asked Questions

What is FedPromptly?

FedPromptly is a builder ecosystem where people turn ideas into software. It brings together practical learning, tools, open development, experiments, and community pathways.

Is it a course?

Not exactly. Learning is one pathway, but the goal is broader: help people make and share real artifacts.

Do I need to be an experienced developer?

No. The intended first step is small. A static page, tiny script, automation, prompt experiment, or documented idea can be a valid beginning.

What can I do today?

Choose one small project, open a starter template, change one thing, and write down what happened. If the project catalog is available, pick a project marked Starter or Building.

Is everything listed already live?

No. Status labels matter. Concept, Planned, Building, Released, and Archived mean different things. Verify links and last-reviewed dates.

How can I contribute?

Contribute code, docs, content, design feedback, project examples, accessibility fixes, moderation, or a thoughtful question. Open a focused issue or pull request.

How can I support FedPromptly?

You can share a project, help another builder, contribute documentation, or use a verified support link. Financial support options are subject to provider and legal review.

Why is there an age gate around subscription embeds?

The current front-end experience asks visitors to confirm they are 18 or older before displaying the requested subscription embeds. This is only a user-interface gate, not proof of identity, legal eligibility, or compliance. Review docs/payment-and-age-gate.md before enabling live billing.

How do I report a security issue?

Do not post secrets or exploit details in a public issue. Follow SECURITY.md and use the private contact configured by the maintainers.
