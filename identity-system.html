FedPromptly / community guide

Community Identity System

A practical, low-pressure way to participate in the FedPromptly ecosystem.

Recognition should describe useful behavior

FedPromptly identity helps people understand how someone participates, not turn contribution into a popularity contest.

Useful signals

  • Projects shipped or documented
  • Helpful answers and thoughtful reviews
  • Accessibility, moderation, and maintenance work
  • Clear build logs and honest retrospectives

Guardrails

No paid status, follower count, or unverified claim should imply expertise. Recognition must be explainable, reversible, and reviewed for abuse.
