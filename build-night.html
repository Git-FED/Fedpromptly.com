Build Night

A practical, low-pressure way to participate in the FedPromptly ecosystem.

A small rhythm for making progress

Build Night is a 60-minute shared work block for people who want company while they make something.

The format

  1. Welcome — 5 minutes. Say what you are working on and what kind of help you want.
  2. Show-and-tell — 10 minutes. Share a current artifact, including rough edges.
  3. Focused build — 30 minutes. Work quietly, ask for help in context, and keep the scope small.
  4. Close — 15 minutes. Share what changed and name the next action.

Asynchronous version

Post your goal, a starting state, a short update, and the next step.
