Project Review Workflow

  1. Confirm the submitter owns or may share the project.
  2. Check that the live link and repository link resolve.
  3. Confirm title, description, category, status, builder attribution, and last-reviewed date.
  4. Check for malware, deceptive claims, scraped content, exposed secrets, and unsafe download instructions.
  5. Ask for clarification rather than silently rewriting a builder's story.
  6. Publish with the appropriate status: Starter, Experiment, Building, Released, or Archived.
  7. Give the builder a correction path and record the reviewer.

A showcase is not an endorsement. Say so clearly near the submission flow.
