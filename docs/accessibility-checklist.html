Accessibility Checklist

Structure

  • One meaningful h1 per page.
  • Heading levels follow hierarchy.
  • Navigation, main, and footer landmarks are present.
  • Links describe their destination.
  • Buttons are buttons; links are links.

Keyboard

  • Tab order follows visual order.
  • Focus is visible against the dark background.
  • Age gate can be completed without a mouse.
  • The gate does not trap focus or leave hidden controls reachable.
  • Escape behavior is defined if a dismissible modal is introduced.

Visual

  • Text and controls meet a reviewed contrast target.
  • Neon effects never carry meaning alone.
  • Hover interactions have keyboard/focus equivalents.
  • Motion can be reduced or disabled.
  • Text remains readable when browser zoom is increased.

Content

  • Images have useful alt text or are marked decorative.
  • Video includes captions and a transcript.
  • Payment and age-gate explanations are understandable without jargon.
  • Error messages tell the user what to do next.
